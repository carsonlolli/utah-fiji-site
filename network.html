How It Works

An intro, not another app

No feed, no inbox to check. The network does one job: it gets the right two people talking, with both of them on board.

01

Opt in once

Parents and alumni add their industry, city and how they're willing to help. Nobody is listed without saying yes.

02

Find the right person

Brothers search by industry, company or city and send a short, specific request.

03

They choose

The request arrives by email with two buttons: "Happy to help" or "Not right now." No login needed.

04

Take it offline

On a yes, both of you get one warm intro email. From there it's your call, your text, your coffee.

Who It's For

Three ways in

Brothers

Ask the parent in finance how they got their first job. Find an alum in Denver before you move. Get a résumé read by someone who hires.

Parents

Share what you've learned with the men your son calls brothers. Help as often or as rarely as you like, and say no anytime.

Alumni

Stay close to the chapter you helped build. A single coffee with a junior can change where he lands.

Privacy

Your details stay yours

Members are approved by chapter officers. Phone numbers and emails stay hidden until you accept a specific request, and only that brother sees them.
